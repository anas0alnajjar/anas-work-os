01Field to decision

A complete information workflow—not a collection of disconnected tools.

The strongest system starts with the operation: who collects the data, how quality is checked, what must remain traceable and which decision the final output must support.

FIELD / 01

Collect data around the real workflow

Design forms and collection routines around the people, locations, constraints and follow-up actions that exist in practice.

  • KoBoToolbox
  • Interviews & surveys
  • Field-team guidance
QUALITY / 02

Make quality visible and actionable

Use validation rules, duplicate checks, review queues and feedback loops so errors are found early and returned with clear action.

  • Validation rules
  • Review & feedback
  • Traceable corrections
SYSTEM / 03

Turn records into an operational system

Connect cases, people, activities, documents and permissions in a structure that supports daily work and preserves history.

  • Data models
  • Roles & permissions
  • Case history
AUTOMATION / 04

Remove repetition without losing control

Automate synchronization, comparisons, attachments, PDFs and notifications while keeping checkpoints, logs and failure reporting.

  • Python / PHP
  • REST APIs
  • Audit outputs
REPORTING / 05

Build outputs people can actually use

Shape case documents, operational summaries and dashboards around recurring review questions—not around decorative charts.

  • Power BI / Excel
  • Management summaries
  • Case-level PDFs
DECISION / 06

Connect information to the next action

Surface workload, gaps, exceptions and follow-up priorities so teams can act with a clearer view of what is happening.

  • Operational visibility
  • Follow-up priorities
  • Decision support
